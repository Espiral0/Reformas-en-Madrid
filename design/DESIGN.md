## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the authority, architectural refinement, and grounded trust of an established high-end residential renovation studio in Madrid. Moving away from utilitarian contractor aesthetics, it adopts an editorial, Mediterranean architectural language: structured, tactile, and calm. 

The aesthetic marries **warm minimalism** with tactile craft. Generous negative space, editorial typography scaling, and soft stone-like tonal layers communicate 20 years of bespoke structural mastery without visual clutter. The experience should evoke confidence, discretion, and effortless execution for high-value homeowners seeking comprehensive property transformations.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Madrilenian architectural materials: fired clay brick, aged limestone, deep slate, and courtyard foliage.

- **Primary (`#C25E3E` - Warm Terracotta):** Used for primary conversion triggers, quote estimates, and focused brand highlights. It provides warmth and visual urgency without the harshness of generic warning reds.
- **Secondary (`#3B533E` - Castilian Olive):** Used for architectural tags, secondary confirmation states, guarantees, and eco/material badges.
- **Tertiary & Typography (`#1E2124` - Deep Slate Charcoal):** Governs all body copy, primary headings, and high-contrast structural framing. Replaces pure black to retain warmth against pale backdrops.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF8F5` & `#F3EFEA`):** Defines the default canvas and stacked surface containers, eliminating stark clinical white in favor of a curated limestone gallery feel.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `border-subtle` (`#E4DFD7`): Structural separation lines and card borders.
  - `whatsapp` (`#25D366`): Dedicated solely to live WhatsApp consultation and instant chat floating elements. Never repurpose for general success indicators.

## Typography

The type system balances architectural geometry with refined legibility.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Modern geometric cuts with balanced proportions create impactful project narratives and high-end positioning. Headlines should maintain tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and generous line heights to preserve editorial elegance.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Highly readable at varied sizes with gentle humanist terminals. Body text relies on open leading (`lineHeight: 1.6 - 1.7`) over the warm off-white canvas to ensure comfortable reading during detailed cost and process evaluations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid within a responsive 12-column system, maxing out at `1280px` for optimal reading and visual containment.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Section pacing relies heavily on `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` for dramatic, uncluttered transitions between project galleries, methodology, and conversion forms.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex dual-form sections stack sequentially.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Sticky action barriers reserve a bottom inset padding to avoid overlaps with the OS home bar and persistent WhatsApp triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on low-contrast structural outlines combined with diffuse, warm-tinted ambient shadows rather than stark digital blurs.

- **Surface Levels:** 
  - Level 0 (Base): `#FAF8F5`
  - Level 1 (Card/Container): `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFEA` paired with a 1px solid border in `#E4DFD7`.
  - Level 2 (Sticky Headers & Modals): Solid `#FAF8F5` with 92% opacity and 12px backdrop blur.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - Card Rest: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.04)`
  - Hover / Interactive: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.08)`
  - Floating CTAs: `0px 8px 24px -2px rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.25)` for primary actions, or `0px 8px 24px -2px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.3)` for WhatsApp interactions.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft modernism with stable architectural discipline. Standard interactive elements employ `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), while parent cards, portfolio previews, and modal containers use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) or `rounded-2xl` (2rem / 32px). Pills are reserved strictly for status chips, micro-guarantees, and circular floating triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta):** Solid `#C25E3E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg`, `rounded-lg` padding: 14px 28px. Subtle lift on hover with `rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.2)` shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Olive/Outline):** 1.5px solid `#3B533E` border, `#3B533E` text, transparent background. Transitions to `#3B533E` fill with `#FFFFFF` text on hover.
- **WhatsApp Floating Button:** Circular 60px container, `#25D366` solid fill, white icon, pinned `1.5rem` from bottom-right with an ambient green shadow.

### Cards & Case Studies
- Containers feature `#FFFFFF` fill over the `#FAF8F5` base, wrapped in a 1px `#E4DFD7` border. Corner radius set to `rounded-xl`.
- Renovation transformation cards include integrated "Before/After" pills using the Olive secondary palette and subtle cross-fades.

### Input Fields & Estimation Forms
- Text fields utilize `#F3EFEA` background, inset border `1px solid #E4DFD7`, and 16px corner radius.
- On focus: Background shifts to `#FFFFFF`, border shifts to `#C25E3E` (1.5px), accompanied by a soft terracotta focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.12)`).
- Labels sit outside input bounds in `label-md` `#1E2124`.

### Chips & Trust Indicators
- Trust tags (e.g., "20 Años de Garantía", "Presupuesto Cerrado") use pill shapes (`rounded-full`), padded with 6px 14px, using `#F3EFEA` background and `#3B533E` text with small inline linear icons.

### Sticky Header
- Slim 72px height, pinned to top with frosted glass styling (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), separated by a 1px border `#E4DFD7`. Contains quick telephone access and high-contrast consultation booking CTA.
